# My 24 Hours: Work-Life Balance Sheet

A free, single-page tool that shows how you spend your 24 hours, gives you a work-life balance score out of 100, personal guidance and a 5-day mini challenge. Built for the Toastmasters International Pathways Level 4 project "Leading Without Losing Life", and useful for anyone.

**Live page:** https://venkatbanday.github.io/my-24-hours/

## What it does

- Enter hours for 10 activities on a typical day (work or study, commute, sleep, meals, family, exercise, club or volunteering, hobbies, rest, other).
- Live donut chart and a total check that must reach exactly 24 hours.
- Scorecard out of 100 with a rating: Thriving, Healthy, Stretched, Out of balance, Warning zone.
- Score by area (sleep, family, exercise, you time, workload, meals) with comments and actions.
- Top 3 priorities, personal notes (leader check, perception check, where you compensate), and a comparison of your day against a healthy day.
- A 5-day mini challenge for your weakest area, plus Copy my result and Print or save as PDF.
- Quick-start examples: Working day, Not working, Leader with extra roles.
- Works on phones and computers, in light and dark mode.

## How to use

1. Open the page, pick an example day or type your own hours.
2. Make the total exactly 24 hours.
3. Tick where you compensate when time is short, and rate your balance from 1 to 10.
4. Read your scorecard and start the 5-day challenge.

## Scoring (100 points)

| Area | Points | Aim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sleep | 25 | 7 to 9 hours |
| Family and friends | 20 | 2+ hours |
| Exercise and health | 15 | 30+ minutes |
| Rest and hobbies ("you" time) | 15 | 1+ hour |
| Workload (work, commute, roles) | 15 | 9 hours or less |
| Meals | 10 | 1+ hour |

Ratings: 85 to 100 Thriving, 70 to 84 Healthy, 50 to 69 Stretched, 30 to 49 Out of balance, 0 to 29 Warning zone.

This is a simple guide based on commonly recommended levels. It is not a medical assessment. If you are worried about your health, please see a doctor.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. Entries are saved only on your own device (local storage) and you can clear them with "Clear everything".

## Publish it yourself (GitHub Pages)

1. Create a public repository, for example `my-24-hours`.
2. Upload `index.html` and this `README.md`.
3. Go to Settings, then Pages, choose Deploy from a branch, main, / (root), and Save.
4. Your page appears at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/my-24-hours/` within a couple of minutes.

## Customise

It is one HTML file with no dependencies. Scoring thresholds are in the `score()` function, and the example days are in `PRESETS`. Colours are CSS variables at the top, based on the Toastmasters palette.
