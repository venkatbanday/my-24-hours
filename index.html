Good to know

Simple facts behind the scorecard.

Sleep comes firstMost adults need at least 7 hours a night. It is the first thing to protect when life gets busy.
Burnout is realThe World Health Organization describes burnout as an occupational phenomenon linked to chronic workplace stress, not a medical diagnosis.
Small beats bigTen minutes of walking or phone-free talk, done daily, beats a big plan that never happens.
People matterRegular, short contact with family and friends protects your wellbeing more than rare, long catch-ups.
